# Vision — Seeing Clearly in a World Shaped by AI

**Mission:** teach people to see clearly in a world shaped by AI.

Not by lecturing. By letting people *live* the failure first, *practice* the defense second, and *name* the concept last. The suite is web-native, gamified, and built for the general public — culturally grounded in the Philippines, readable everywhere.

## The three pillars

Knowledge alone doesn't survive contact with a confident voice on the phone. Experience alone doesn't generalize. Concepts alone don't feel like anything. The three pillars compound:

### 1. PLAY — Story (felt experience)
The **BLACKGLASS phone-anthology**: Rashomon-structured interactive fiction where you live AI-powered deception from inside different family members' phones — and each phone is itself a different way of being fooled. No scores, no lessons on the wall. The form is the lesson.

### 2. TRAIN — Skills (gut instinct)
**Scam Radar**: a scam-readiness trainer. Where the story makes you the observer, the trainer makes you the target — in a safe sandbox. Rapid-fire feed triage (judge Scam / Legit / Verify first), then narrative case files where you inspect artifacts, gather clues, and choose responses with consequences. Builds the reflexes: pause, verify through official channels, hang up, report, protect your family.

### 3. LEARN — Concepts (framework)
The **literacy games**: Human-in-the-Loop (trust calibration), Bias Bounty (algorithmic bias patterns), Hallucination Hunt (verification skills), Risk Assessment Protocol (NIST AI RMF governance). Conceptual frameworks, made playable.

## Principles

1. **Invisible pedagogy.** The mechanic carries the lesson; the debrief names it. Learning lives in what the player *did*, not what we told them.
2. **Dignity for the fooled.** Characters are whole people, never cautionary cartoons. Everyone is foolable; that's the point.
3. **No functional harm.** We depict deception; we never produce working scam templates, real numbers, or real identities (`world/guardrails.md`).
4. **Accessibility is the product.** Mobile-first, keyboard-only playable, screen-reader supported, reduced-motion safe, offline after first visit, works on slow phones.
5. **Diegetic graphics.** We generate only what characters *see* — convincing AI artifacts with deliberately planted tells that become the teaching surface in the examine layer.
6. **One engine, many stories.** New games run on the shared phone/beat engine; content is data; the form stays testable.
7. **Local-first trust.** Progress lives in the browser (`cgAI_` localStorage keys). No accounts, no tracking, no servers with player data.

## What "Blackglass" means

Glass distorts what's behind it and shows you yourself. Scams are black glass: convincing surfaces over absent realities. The project's job is to teach the tilt of the head — the pause, the second look — that reveals the glass for glass.
