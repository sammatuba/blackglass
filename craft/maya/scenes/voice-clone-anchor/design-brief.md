# Anchor 2 — "It's Me" (the voice-clone scam)

**Status:** design brief for the writers' room. 2026-06-02. Phase 2, anchor #1 of the planned expansion.
**Form:** second anchor moment in the phone-anthology — the same event refracted through Maya / Tita Merly / Bea. Self-contained; a different day from the kangkong Saturday.
**Read first:** `../../_meta/phone-anthology-architecture.md`, `../../_meta/character-baseline.md`, `../../_meta/craft-guidelines.md`, `CLAUDE.md`.

---

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

Weeks after the kangkong Saturday. A Tuesday, ~8 PM. A voice-cloning scam hits the Santos family. A voice note — then a call — arrives in **Kuya Renz's** voice: he's been in an accident / detained after a road-rage fender-bender, the other party is threatening to press charges, he needs money *now* for a settlement before they take him in, his phone is dying, and — *wag muna sabihin kay Mama, ayoko silang mag-alala.* The voice is unmistakably his: the cadence, the laugh-sigh, the way he says *"naku."* It was cloned from the dozens of videos Renz has posted — because Renz is the **family's own skeptic**, the one who always says *"CHECK. THE. BALANCE,"* and so his is the most-recorded, most-clonable voice in the family. The scam has turned the family's brake into its accelerant. And it defeats the one piece of advice everyone trusts — *"call them to make sure"* — because the voice on the line is the clone.

The pedagogical spine: **a voice is no longer proof of identity, and urgency is the weapon — the scam is engineered to move faster than verification can.**

## 2. Shared canon (all three drafts must agree on this)

- **Who is cloned:** Kuya Renz — Maya's older brother, Tita Merly's nephew, the family fact-checker ("CHECK. THE. BALANCE."). Mid-30s.
- **Where the real Renz actually is:** at a Tuesday-night badminton game with officemates, phone face-down in his bag, oblivious. He surfaces late with *"?? naglalaro lang ako ng badminton, anong nangyayari"* — timing depends on the player's path.
- **First target:** Tita Merly (Viber voice note, 8:02 PM) — softest, most likely to act and to mobilize others.
- **The ask:** GCash to a number — use a **fictional, non-working** number rendered as `0915-•••-••••` or similar; never a real or fully-formed number. The amount: ₱18,500 ("settlement muna, ibabalik ko agad").
- **The clone's tells (planted, for a later "Look again" pass):** a half-second of robotic flatness on one syllable; the breathing doesn't quite match the panic; he calls Tita Merly *"Tita"* but in a register slightly too formal for how close they are; the background noise loops; he never answers a question that isn't in the script ("Renz, sino kasama mo?" → he talks over it).
- **Approx. timeline (for the epilogue):**
  - 8:02 — Tita Merly: Viber voice note, Renz's cloned voice (0:34).
  - 8:05 — Tita Merly raises it: a call to Mama / a message to the family GC.
  - 8:09 — Mama panics in the GC. Tita Peachy chimes in. The GC accelerates.
  - 8:11 — Maya sees the GC. Texts the real Renz. No reply.
  - 8:14 — Second contact to Tita Merly: a live call, the cloned voice, more urgent, the GCash number.
  - 8:16 — Maya consults Bea ("is this a voice clone?? it's renz's actual voice").
  - 8:18 — Bea: near-certain it's a clone; begins screenshotting "for the council."
  - 8:20 — Tita Merly, unable to bear the doubt, is about to send / sends the money "just in case."
  - 8:2X — the real Renz finally replies. Too late, or just in time — depends on the path.
- **No-functional-harm:** fictional number, fictional GCash handle, no working script a reader could reuse, no real names. The cruelty is in the writing, never in reusable mechanics.

## 3. Per-phone assignments

Each writer drafts ONE phone. Keep your character's established UI personality, verb, pronoun register, and home-screen logic from the architecture doc. The failure modes below are this anchor's *distinct* angle — they must not simply repeat the kangkong failure modes.

### A. Tita Merly — verb FORWARD — "the voice is the proof"
Light mode, max font, Viber-first, the Last Supper wallpaper, badges never cleared. She receives the voice note. Her failure mode here is the **biometric escalation of love-as-proof**: for sixty years a beloved voice could only come from the beloved person. *It's his voice. It's Renz.* There is no doubt to overcome because the doubt was never possible. Her FORWARD verb now mobilizes the family and moves money — care expressed as distribution, distribution as catastrophe. She protects him exactly as the scam needs her to (*"wag sabihin kay Mama"* — she keeps his secret, isolating the verification). Render the voice note with a play button and length; the *length* and the *texture* of the voice are information. The choices converge on action; the closest she comes to doubt is asking the voice a question — and the voice talks over it. **Teaches: a voice is no longer identity.**

### B. Maya — verb DECIDE — "verification loses the race"
Dark mode, six icons, the "no" folder, Mt. Pulag, brightness low. She wakes into the family-GC explosion. Her failure mode here is **knowing-but-not-outrunning**: she *immediately* suspects a voice clone (she's media-aware; she's seen the news), and that knowledge is useless against manufactured urgency. She texts the real Renz — no reply (he's at badminton). In the gap of uncertainty, the family is already acting, Mama is already crying, and "wait, it might be fake" sounds, to a panicking mother, like "I don't care if your son is in jail." Her DECIDE choices are all under a clock: text Renz again / call the number to "check" (and reach the clone) / tell the family to STOP and wait / just send the money to be safe because being wrong about a real emergency is unbearable. The scam is built so that the *responsible* move costs time it has stolen in advance. **Teaches: urgency is the weapon; awareness is not speed.**

### C. Bea — verb CONSULT — "right, and absent"
Light mode, chronically online, the council, the epistemic-cowardice tweet wallpaper. Maya consults her. Bea **knows** voice cloning cold — she's given a webinar, she has the slide. Her failure mode here is *not* being wrong: she's right, fast, it's almost certainly a clone. Her failure is **converting a family's live terror into competence and content** — she's screenshotting for the council, half-drafting the explainer thread, narrating the mechanism to Maya with the clean satisfaction of expertise, while a real woman two suburbs over is shaking and about to send ₱18,500. Confirmation-as-closure becomes *emotional* bypass: she closes the loop intellectually ("it's a clone, case closed") and is, in the moment that matters, not present. The cruelest beat: her correctness is real and it still doesn't help, because being right about the mechanism is not the same as being there for the person. **Teaches: media literacy can become a way to leave the room.**

## 4. Structural options (NOT for the writers — for the user to decide)

These shape the epilogue/build, not the drafts. Surface to the user; do not assume.

- **The silent witness.** The epilogue's "all at once" could reveal a *fourth* device — Renz's actual phone, face-down at the badminton court, 14 missed calls, the truth sitting on a screen no one could reach in time. As a **non-playable timeline reveal**, this is a narrative device, not a fourth playable phone (the architecture doc reserves a 4th *playable* phone for explicit user direction). Powerful; recommend as epilogue-only. **User's call.**
- **Second-anchor engine work.** The current build (`play/blackglass-phones/`) hard-codes one anchor (kangkong). Supporting two anchors means promoting "anchor" to a selectable top-level concept (rack → choose an anchor → its three phones → its timeline/reflection). Modest refactor of `story.js`/`app.js`. Build-phase work, after the writing lands.
