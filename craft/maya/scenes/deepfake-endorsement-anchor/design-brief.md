# Anchor 3 — "GUARANTEED" (the deepfake-endorsement scam)

**Status:** design brief for the writers' room. Phase 2, anchor #3.
**Form:** a third Rashomon anchor — the same event refracted through Maya / Tita Merly / Bea. A Sunday afternoon.
**Read first:** `../../_meta/phone-anthology-architecture.md`, `../../_meta/character-baseline.md`, `../../_meta/craft-guidelines.md`, and the two prior anchors (kangkong = `phone-anthology-architecture.md` §5; voice-clone = `../voice-clone-anchor/`).

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

A Sunday afternoon. A **deepfake video** is circulating on Facebook and TikTok: **Tito Mike Velasco** — the beloved (fictional) evening-news anchor every lola in the country trusts, the steady voice that has read the 6pm news for thirty years — appears in a clean studio-looking clip announcing a new "government-partnered" investment program, **AngatPinas Invest**: *guaranteed 30% returns in 30 days, SEC-approved daw, para sa mga senior, OFW, at maliliit na negosyante.* It is not Tito Mike. It is his face and his voice, generated. The video reaches Tita Merly through her prayer-group friend, and within the hour it is in the Santos family GC, attached to a hope: *para sa medical fund ni Papa.* 

The pedagogical spine here is the one the first two anchors don't cover: **synthetic media collapses "seeing is believing" — and its deeper damage is the *liar's dividend*: once everyone knows a face can be faked, real faces and real warnings stop being believed too.**

## 2. Shared canon (all three drafts must agree)

- **The trusted face (fictional):** Tito Mike Velasco, ~60s, the country's most-trusted news anchor, grandfatherly, "parang kapamilya." Invent freely; resembles no real person.
- **The scam vehicle:** AngatPinas Invest — a fictional app. The pitch: "government-partnered," "SEC-approved," **guaranteed 30% in 30 days**, min deposit ₱5,000. The fake site/app handle rendered as `angatpinas-invest.app` (fictional, non-working).
- **The deepfake's tells (planted, for a later "Look again"):** the mouth runs slightly behind certain words; an unnatural blink rhythm; the lower-third "government seal" is a subtly garbled/melted official logo; the phrase "guaranteed returns" itself (no real investment guarantees); the testimonial faces in the b-roll are AI (too-smooth, asymmetric).
- **Approx. timeline (Sunday):**
  - 2:14 PM — the deepfake lands in Tita Merly's FB feed (shared by Auntie Linda from prayer group).
  - 2:30 — Tita forwards it to the Santos Family GC: *"Para kay Papa. Si Tito Mike pa ang nag-endorso."*
  - 2:48 — relatives react; Tita Peachy is in; a cousin asks how to join.
  - 3:20 — Maya sees the GC. Knows on sight it's a deepfake.
  - 3:40 — Maya consults Bea; Bea debunks expertly.
  - 4:05 — a relative has already "reserved a slot" / is asking for the deposit steps.
  - later — **the liar's-dividend beat:** when Maya/Bea post the debunk, the reply is *"baka totoo naman, bakit ka negative"* — and worse, a relative now waves off a **real** SEC advisory (or a real hospital bill reminder) with *"baka deepfake din yan."* The fakes have poisoned trust in the genuine.
- **No-functional-harm:** fictional anchor, fictional app, fictional handle, no real SEC/government branding reproduced working, no real investment instructions. The scam is depicted, never operable.

## 3. Per-phone assignments (distinct from the first two anchors)

### A. Tita Merly — FORWARD — "his face, so it's true"
She trusts Tito Mike the way she trusts the news itself — thirty years of him in her living room at 6pm. The deepfake hijacks *parasocial + institutional* trust: it isn't a stranger's link (kangkong) or a loved one's voice (voice-clone) — it's the trusted public face plus the word "government." Her failure: the dream of provision (Papa's medical fund, the daughter's padala stretched thin) makes the guaranteed return a blessing she's been praying for, and Tito Mike's face is all the verification a blessing needs. Render the deepfake as a real video beat she watches. The seniors are the target on purpose. **Teaches: deepfakes weaponize trusted faces; "seeing is believing" was the whole vulnerability.**

### B. Maya — DECIDE — "you know, and you almost wish you didn't"
She clocks it instantly — the mouth, the blink, the impossible 30%. But she's a freelancer between gigs, and a small, ugly part of her *wishes it were real,* which is the first time her skepticism has had to fight her own want. Worse: to say "it's fake" in the GC is to be the killjoy who takes away Papa's hope in front of everyone — the social cost of skepticism. And the liar's dividend turns on her: her own certainty wobbles ("what if I'm wrong and they miss something real"). **Teaches: motivated reasoning lowers the verification threshold, and being the skeptic costs you socially — the scam is engineered around both.**

### C. Bea — CONSULT — "right, outspread, and disbelieved"
Her exact professional beat — she debunks it cleanly, fast, with receipts (no SEC registration, the seal is fake, guarantees are illegal). And it does not matter, in three escalating ways: (1) the debunk reaches a tenth of the deepfake's spread; (2) it's *tribally discounted* — "you're just being negative / mayaman ka kasi"; (3) the deepest cut — the liar's dividend — a relative now dismisses a **real** advisory as "baka deepfake din," and Bea realizes the scam's real product was not the fake video but the *doubt* it installed in everything afterward. **Teaches: the liar's dividend — synthetic media's true damage is the collapse of belief in the genuine; a correct debunk can lose to a poisoned epistemic commons.**
