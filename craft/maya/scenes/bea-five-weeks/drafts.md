# Arc — "FIVE WEEKS" — workshop draft + editorial

**Status:** drafted 2026-06-03 (`blackglass-writer`). Built into the engine as a single-phone, time-compressed anchor (`fiveweeks`). Full verbatim in the agent transcript; key beats below.
**Brief:** [`design-brief.md`](design-brief.md). **Form:** one phone (Bea), five frames = five weeks. Drift, not fork.

## Editorial synthesis

The project's first non-Rashomon piece, and the counterpoint the acute scams need: **no lie, no moment, just a feed paying you a little more each time you're a little less careful.** Bea — the media-literacy expert — is entrained by engagement incentives until, by week five, she gives a workshop on *not trusting sources that confirm you* while having spent five weeks trusting exactly those. Awareness is not immunity.

**The five weeks (the drift):**
1. **The hit** — an accidental sharp tweet outperforms anything careful she's posted. The clean small dopamine. *Choice: keep the caveats / cut them.*
2. **The trim** — the feed serves more of what performed; nuance underperforms, so the hedges come off. *"The careful version is true. The sharp version is shareable."*
3. **The purity test** — a pile-on on someone "insufficiently pure" (a hedged researcher) has the numbers; joining feels like accountability. *Choice: sit it out / quote-tweet the pile.*
4. **The hall of agreement** — she mutes (not blocks — "curation") the accounts that complicate; the feed goes quiet; she mistakes the quiet for consensus.
5. **The webinar** — "Seeing Clearly: A Media Literacy Workshop." Slide: *do not trust a source just because it confirms you.* 73 people nod. *"You taught them to see clearly. You do not see yourself."* The wallpaper tweet — "epistemic cowardice is choosing civility over clarity" — turns from courage to alibi. **The recognition is available; it is never forced.**

**Craft moves:** week headers + follower/engagement metrics as the ellipsis grammar (§4); choices are tonal not topological (§2); quiet drift tags `cut_for_sharpness` / `hedges_cut` / `joined_the_pile` / `muted_the_complications` accumulate with no visible stat (§3); the refusal — she never admits in the moment she's being shaped.

## Epilogue — "What five weeks did" (the drift timeline)
The same UI as the Rashomon timelines, but the rows are weeks, and the through-line is the follower count climbing while the nuance drains. Close: *No step was a lie. The sum is a stranger. The feed never argued with you — it paid you, a little, each time you were a little less careful, until less careful felt like clarity.*

## Reflection
- **Bea · Awareness is not immunity.** She could name every mechanism of capture while being captured by it.
- **Coda:** *Drift has no moment you can point to — that's what makes it drift. The other stories happen TO people in an afternoon. This one happens AS you, over weeks, and the only proof is that the person at the end would not recognize the person at the start.*
